How to Renew
  1. Note your Renewal ID above — it's auto-generated from your Barangay name and the year being licensed.
  2. Settle payment of the annual license fee with RCV Digital Solutions.
  3. Email rcvinitiatives@gmail.com with your proof of payment and the details listed below.
  4. RCV Digital Solutions will email you a License Key once payment is confirmed.
  5. Paste the License Key above and click Activate License Key. Your license will then be valid through December 31 of the licensed year.
Please include in your renewal email:
1. Renewal ID (shown above)
2. Barangay name, municipality, and province
3. Full name and position of the requesting officer (e.g., Barangay Secretary or Admin user)
4. Contact number and email address
5. Proof of payment (screenshot or reference / transaction number)
6. Software version (shown below)
